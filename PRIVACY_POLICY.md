# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NfsBrowser ("we," "us," or "our") handles your data when you use our mobile browser application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## 1. Data We Do Not Collect

As a core principle, we do not monitor or track your internet activity. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your:

* Browsing history
* Search queries
* Downloaded files
* Passwords or form data

All of this information remains stored locally on your device and is never sent to our servers.

## 2. Local Device Storage

To function properly as a web browser, the App uses your device's local storage (via Android's native WebView) to save:

1. Cookies and session data required by websites you visit.
2. Web cache to load pages faster.
3. Files you explicitly choose to download.

You can clear this local data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings.

## 3. Third-Party Websites and Services

When you navigate to a website or use a search engine (e.g., Google, Bing) through our App, those third parties may collect your IP address, cookies, and browsing data. This App does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the websites you visit.

## 4. Permissions

The App may request certain Android device permissions (such as Storage access) solely to enable core browser features, like downloading files or uploading images to a website. We do not use these permissions to access your personal files for our own use.

## 5. Data Security

We rely on the built-in security features of the Android WebView engine to help protect your browsing sessions. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and you are responsible for avoiding malicious websites and securing your device.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at: [novfensec@protonmail.com](mailto:novfensec@protonmail.com).
